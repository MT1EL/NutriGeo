# NutriGeo — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 1, 2026_

We value your privacy. This document explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use NutriGeo.

## 1. What data we collect

We collect data necessary to provide the service: name, email, age, weight, height, meal logs, and activity statistics. We also collect basic device information (operating system version, app version) for diagnostic purposes.

## 2. How we use the data

Your data is used to give personalized recommendations, calculate calorie and macro targets, and track your progress over time. We do not use your data for advertising profiling.

## 3. Sharing

We never sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing. Sharing only happens when required by law, or with service providers who help us operate the app (hosting, crash reporting, payment processing) under strict data-processing agreements.

## 4. Security

We use high-grade encryption both in transit (TLS) and at rest. Your account is protected by a password, and credentials are stored using industry-standard hashing.

## 5. Your rights

You can request export, update, or deletion of your data at any time. You can also delete your account directly from within the app: **Profile → Settings → Delete account**. For other requests, contact us at **privacy@nutrigeo.ge**.

## 6. Children

NutriGeo is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. International transfers

Our servers may be located outside Georgia. By using the app you consent to your data being processed in those jurisdictions, which provide equivalent protection.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll notify you via the app or email. Continued use of the service after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: **privacy@nutrigeo.ge**
